# Privacy Policy for ShrinkToken

**Last Updated:** July 2026

OpenArc ("we," "us," or "our") built the ShrinkToken extension as a Free tool. This SERVICE is provided by OpenArc at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any of your data.**

The ShrinkToken extension operates entirely on your local machine (client-side). The text prompts you paste into the extension are processed locally within your browser using JavaScript. We do not use external servers to process your prompts, and none of your text, data, or personal information ever leaves your device.

### 2. Log Data

Since our extension runs completely offline and client-side, we do not collect any Log Data, IP addresses, or browser usage statistics.

### 3. Cookies

The ShrinkToken extension does not use cookies to track users or store personal information. Your token-saving metrics are saved locally in your browser's local storage and are never transmitted to us.

### 4. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising libraries that would compromise your privacy.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
